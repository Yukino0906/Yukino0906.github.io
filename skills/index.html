Skills

I fabricate and characterize thin-film devices, model their measured behavior, and design circuits that use them.

Device fabrication

I use pulsed laser deposition (PLD) and sputtering to grow thin films. My patterning experience includes photoresist spin coating, maskless direct-write lithography on the Heidelberg MLA150, and wet and dry etching.

Thin-film growth and 1S1R integration →

Characterization & test automation

I use X-ray diffraction, ellipsometry, AFM and TEM sample preparation to investigate thin-film structure. For electrical testing, I work with probe stations, SMUs, PMUs and oscilloscopes.

I build modular DC and pulse measurement workflows with NI PXIe and VISA-based instruments, including automated data logging and analysis.

Cleanroom work and automated electrical testing →

IC & hardware design

I design and simulate analog circuits in Cadence Virtuoso and Spectre, model device behavior in Verilog-A, and develop digital hardware in Verilog and SystemVerilog.

Digital implementation and timing
Cadence Genus, Innovus and Tempus
Synthesis and design for test
Synopsys Fusion Compiler and TestMAX
PCB design
Altium Designer and EasyEDA
Analog bias design → RTL to layout →

Programming & data analysis

I use MATLAB and Python to extract device parameters from measurements and evaluate programming accuracy. My other tools include C/C++, Origin, SPICE and MNSIM.
